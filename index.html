Process

My development process

The same five steps for every project, small or large.

01

Discover

Understand the idea, the people using it, and what the project actually needs to do.

02

Plan

Define structure, functionality, and the technical approach before writing code.

03

Design

Work out a clean visual direction and a layout that holds up across screen sizes.

04

Develop

Build the site and its functionality, piece by piece, testing as I go.

05

Refine

Test, debug, optimize, and polish the final experience before it ships.
